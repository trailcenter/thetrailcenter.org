TRAIL Center Insight · August 2026

Welcome to the TRAIL Center

Turning trauma knowledge into action across people, organizations, communities, and systems.

The TRAIL Center was created from a simple conviction: understanding trauma matters, but understanding alone is not enough. Knowledge must become action—through research that asks better questions, education that is useful in practice, advocacy that centers dignity, support that makes connection possible, and systems change that alters the conditions shaping people’s lives.

TRAIL stands for Trauma, Research, Advocacy, Impact, and Learning. These commitments come together through four connected branches. TRAIL Lab studies trauma, coping, victimization, justice involvement, practitioner experiences, and life-course outcomes. TRAIL Speaks builds advocacy, information, peer connection, resources, and lived-experience-centered programming. TRAIL Academy translates evidence into accessible training, curricula, workshops, and professional learning. TRAIL Forward focuses on institutional improvement through programs, policies, procedures, protocols, and practices.

Our goals reach beyond a single project. We are building pathways for students to learn and lead, helping organizations develop more responsive practices, creating public resources, and laying the groundwork for future initiatives such as Street Reach, youth learning programs, and community-centered research and data collection.

Trauma can shape health, behavior, relationships, education, safety, justice involvement, and opportunity. It can also be misunderstood when systems focus only on a person’s behavior without asking what happened, what conditions surround them, and what support or change is needed. The TRAIL Center works to widen that lens while holding space for accountability, agency, healing, and transformation.

We invite researchers, students, practitioners, survivors, advocates, organizations, and community members to explore the Center, share ideas, collaborate, and help turn trauma knowledge into meaningful change.
