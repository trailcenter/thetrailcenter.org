How to enroll and pay

  • Submit a separate registration form for each child.
  • Use the same parent email address in Stripe and on your registration form.
  • Add the $50 one-time annual registration fee at checkout only if you haven’t already paid it for this child this year. It doesn’t renew automatically.
  • If you’re enrolling in more than one program, complete a separate checkout for each one. Add the annual fee only once per child.

TRAIL Kids Academy will confirm your child’s placement separately. Questions? Email academy@thetrailcenter.org.
