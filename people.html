A message from the director

Dr. Lori L. Hall

Director

“The TRAIL Center was built on the belief that trauma knowledge must move beyond awareness and become meaningful action. Our growing team brings together research, education, advocacy, lived experience, creativity, and systems thinking to build practical pathways toward healing and change. We welcome collaboration with people and organizations who share that commitment.”

— Dr. Lori L. Hall
