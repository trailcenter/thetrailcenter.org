Street Reach · Nourishment and connection

therapy.

therapy. is a proposed mental-health-focused food trailer serving high-quality, nourishing food through a creative menu inspired by mental health, therapy, reflection, and connection. Its guiding message is nourishing the soul. nourishing the body.

The sharp-edged matte-black trailer and white typewriter-style identity create a memorable public space where good food can open the door to conversation. Beyond meals, the project can connect guests with mental-health information, community resources, TRAIL Center programming, and a message that seeking support belongs in everyday life.

  • Quality meals with thoughtful therapy-inspired menu themes
  • Mental-health information and community-resource connections
  • Pop-up outreach at schools, campuses, neighborhoods, and events
  • A welcoming platform for conversation, dignity, and belonging

Street Reach · Care and dignity

Mobile Basic Care Trailer

The Street Reach Mobile Basic Care Trailer is envisioned as a dignified, accessible space that brings essential services directly to people who may face barriers to stable facilities and personal-care resources.

  • Two private showers in safe, clean spaces
  • Two washers and two dryers for laundry and clean-clothing support
  • Hygiene products and basic-care kits available to guests
  • Haircuts and personal-grooming services
  • A Resources & Therapy Connections area for referrals and service navigation
  • Secure phone charging
  • Computer and application assistance for housing, benefits, employment, education, identification, and therapy services

Every element is designed around privacy, respect, human connection, and the belief that basic care and practical support can strengthen safety, confidence, health, access, and healing.

Mentorship and education

Higher Learning Institute

The Higher Learning Institute is a sustained mentorship and education program supporting trauma-affected, justice-impacted, and at-risk teens as they prepare for college and continue through the transition into higher education.

Support begins when a young person enters the program and continues through college. Participants receive consistent mentorship, planning, encouragement, workshops, and courses throughout the year.

  • Individual mentorship and long-term relationship-based support
  • College readiness, applications, financial-aid, and transition guidance
  • Academic, professional, leadership, and life-skills workshops
  • Support navigating trauma, justice involvement, and educational barriers
  • Continued connection and mentoring after college enrollment

Early learning and support

Kids Academy

Kids Academy is an educational and mentorship program that helps children build emotional intelligence, resilience, healthy coping, communication, self-awareness, and supportive relationships.

Through developmentally appropriate activities, mentoring, and trauma-responsive learning, children gain practical tools for understanding emotions, responding to challenges, asking for help, and recognizing their own strengths.

Reentry, reintegration, and healing

TRAIL Farm Reentry Transitional Housing

TRAIL Farm is a proposed residential reentry and recidivism-reduction program for men and women returning to the community after incarceration. The program will combine safe transitional housing, individualized reentry support, meaningful work, education, mentorship, and trauma-responsive care within a supportive farm-based community.

Using a holistic, humanistic approach, TRAIL Farm will help residents address the practical and emotional challenges that can make reintegration difficult. Each participant will work with the program to develop a personalized plan focused on stability, healing, self-sufficiency, healthy relationships, and long-term success.

  • Safe, structured transitional housing for men and women returning from correctional settings
  • Individualized reentry planning, case coordination, and connections to community resources
  • A working farm that builds practical skills, routine, responsibility, teamwork, and connection with nature
  • A farm-to-table restaurant offering culinary training, employment experience, and community engagement
  • Individual and group therapy, peer support, and trauma-responsive behavioral-health services
  • Education, vocational preparation, job-readiness support, financial literacy, and life-skills development
  • Assistance with identification, benefits, healthcare, transportation, employment, and permanent housing
  • Mentorship, restorative relationships, and continued support as residents transition into independent living

Successful reentry requires more than release from incarceration. It requires a safe place to live, meaningful opportunities, reliable relationships, practical resources, and space to heal. TRAIL Farm will create an environment where residents can rebuild stability and purpose while preparing for healthy, independent participation in their families and communities.

Future funding priority: TRAIL Farm is a future program for which the TRAIL Center is seeking funding, land, facilities, and community partners.
